Canopy privacy

Screenshots, recognized screenshot text, meeting notes, to-do lists, headings, links, transcripts, preferences and existing summaries are stored on your Mac. The App Store version stores its library inside its sandbox container. Canopy does not operate an account service, advertising network, analytics service or cloud library. Formatted notes are kept as local editor documents with plain-text and Markdown representations. The document editor and its dependencies are bundled inside the app and do not download scripts or upload notes. Clicking a web link opens your browser; the destination has its own privacy policy.

Meeting audio is processed in memory by Apple's on-device speech engine. No audio recording file is saved or uploaded by Canopy. Apple manages the language model download. Meeting audio captures sound playing on the Mac, which can include unrelated apps. Your microphone is used only when you select a microphone source. Listening starts only when you choose Start listening, and a visible recording indicator remains in the menu bar until it stops.

Canopy's Capture button and Control–Option–S shortcut save the selected screen area directly to its shelf using Apple's ScreenCaptureKit. These captures create no Desktop file or macOS corner thumbnail. The App Store version does not read or change the system screenshot destination or thumbnail preferences. Optional folder detection reads only a folder you choose, copies new macOS screenshots into the shelf and leaves originals in that folder. Ordinary macOS screenshots retain the system's destination and thumbnail behavior. Captures remain in Canopy until discarded. Pending source files in an older app-owned inbox are removed only after the app copy is durably saved. Screenshot text recognition runs on your Mac. Preview edit copies and drag transfer copies are local files.

AI tools are deferred in this version. Ask, AI summaries, Jev analysis and API settings are unavailable. Screenshots, notes and transcripts are not sent to an AI provider. Connection settings, credentials and summary text saved by an older direct version are preserved. This version's ordinary settings do not read or overwrite those credentials. Previously saved API credentials remain in macOS Keychain, outside the library JSON file. No tracking identifier is created and no data is sold by Canopy.

Control and deletion

Stop listening to stop audio capture. Pause screenshot detection or choose a different folder to stop access to the previous folder. The screenshot × removes Canopy's shelf image and its Preview edit copy; copies exported to other apps or folders remain there. Delete a meeting from its menu to remove its notes, transcript and existing summary. Temporary drag transfers are removed after five minutes while the app is running, and stale transfers are removed on the next launch. Credentials retained from a previous version can be managed in macOS Keychain Access.

Local library files are plaintext protected by macOS filesystem permissions, not encrypted by Canopy. macOS backups and copies you export can remain after deletion. Upgrading an older library creates a one-time local backup in the library’s Backups folder before saving the new notebook format. That backup can contain older notes after you remove them from the current notebook. Reveal library in Settings to manage this backup. You can reveal the library in Settings. For publisher contact details and policy updates, use the Privacy Policy and Support links on Canopy's App Store product page.
